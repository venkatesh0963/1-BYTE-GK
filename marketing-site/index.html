Frequently Asked Questions

1 Byte GK is a gamified General Knowledge learning platform designed to make preparation fun, consistent, and highly rewarding through daily challenges and subject-wise series.
Every day, you are presented with a fresh set of 50 mixed General Knowledge questions. Answering them correctly earns you XP and maintains your daily streak.
Complete your daily 50-question challenge to extend your streak by one day. If you miss a day, your streak resets to zero. Consistency is the key to building high streaks!
You earn Experience Points (XP) for every correct answer. Bonus XP is awarded for completing daily challenges and hitting streak milestones. XP determines your rank on the global leaderboard.
We offer a comprehensive curriculum covering History, Polity, Civics, Geography, English, General Aptitude, Science, Computer Awareness, Economics, Current Affairs, Sports, and Art & Culture.
The Normal plan includes access to the 50 daily questions, basic subject series, standard progress tracking, and the basic leaderboard.
The Pro plan unlocks everything! You get unlimited practice, all premium subject series, advanced analytics, full mock tests, previous-year question papers, and the advanced leaderboard.
